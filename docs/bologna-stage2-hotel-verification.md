# Bologna Stage 2 preflight — official hotel verification

**27 October 2026. Read-only research. No application code was changed and
Stage 2 was not implemented.**

Verification of official identity, address and access-related facts for the
four shortlisted hotels, from each hotel's own website.

---

## Headline

**All four hotels were verified from their own official websites.** Two of
them publish property-specific access instructions that are considerably
better than anything Phase 1 had, and **one of them contradicts a Phase 1
assumption outright.**

**The Comune di Bologna's own ZTL rules remain unreadable**, exactly as in
Phase 1. So no ZTL classification in the table below rests on municipal
evidence — only on what each hotel states about itself, or on nothing.

| **Published address** | **Via dell'Indipendenza, 8 — 40121 Bologna, Italy** |

**What the hotel publishes, verbatim, that changes the page:**

> "IMPORTANT: NEVER USE THE ADDRESS OF OUR PROPERTY 'VIA DELL'INDIPENDENZA 8'
> ON YOUR GPS. Via dell'Indipendenza is totally pedestrianized, consequently
> it is mandatory to reach the Hotel side entrance in Via Manzoni 2
> (Restaurant 'I Carracci' entrance)."

> "Set your GPS to 'Via Galliera 23', then continue straight for 200 meters.
> Turn left onto Via Manzoni and follow the road to the end. About 50 meters
> before the end of the street, our valet will be there to welcome you."

> "Via Manzoni is also a pedestrian street, but the Hotel has a municipality
> permission for all our Guests."

> "You are not allowed to drive along Via Indipendenza, Rizzoli, Ugo Bassi,
> Archiginnasio, Piazza San Francesco, Via Antonio Bertoloni and on bus yellow
> lanes."

> "Entrances ZTL to be used to reach us: Via Marconi."

> "Due to the traffic restrictions in the pedestrian area where the hotel is
> located, the return time for your car may be longer than usual."

**Caveat, and it is the important one in this document.** The municipality
permission described is **the hotel's, for its own guests, operated through
its own valet**. It is not evidence that a private-hire vehicle may drive Via
Manzoni, and it must never be presented as our access right. The publishable
facts are: the registered address cannot be driven to; the hotel instructs
arrivals to use the Via Manzoni side entrance; the hotel states it holds a
municipal permission for guests; the hotel names streets on which driving is
not permitted. Whether any particular vehicle may use that route is between
the hotel and the Comune.

## What this changes about the Phase 1 plan

Recorded rather than quietly folded in, per instruction. **Phase 1's findings
are preserved as written; these are corrections on top of them.**

1. **Commercianti's page premise was wrong.** Phase 1 planned "the car
   finishes nearby; the last stretch is on foot". The hotel states a car can
   reach it by an authorised route with its plate registered. The page must be
   re-planned.

2. **The Majestic's page premise was right but incomplete, and far better
   than Phase 1 knew.** The hotel publishes a different vehicle entrance from
   its own address, a named ZTL gate, a named list of prohibited streets and a
   warning that car retrieval takes longer. That is genuinely
   property-specific material of a kind no other page on this site has.

3. **Two of the four ZTL tiers are unsupported.** Phase 1's differentiation
   rested on placing each hotel in one of three tiers. "Outside the ZTL"
   (Excelsior) and "outside the centre" (Savoia) are **inferences**, not
   findings — no municipal source was readable and neither hotel mentions the
   zone.

   **Recommended replacement:** differentiate on **what each hotel itself
   publishes about arriving by car**. That is better evidenced, more
   property-specific, and happens to be sharply different per hotel:

   | Hotel | What it publishes about arriving by car |
   |---|---|
   | Majestic | Do not use our address; use the side entrance; here is the gate; here are the streets you may not drive |
   | Commercianti | Pedestrian area inside the ZTL; call reception; we register your plate; paid garage 07:00–22:00 |
   | Excelsior | Ordinary motorway directions to the door; no restriction mentioned; the airport monorail stops opposite |
   | Savoia | Motorway and beltway exits; 800 spaces; no restriction mentioned |

4. **No distance may be published for any of the four.** Excelsior says 11 km
   to the airport; Savoia says 10 km on one page and 15 minutes on another;
   no authority confirms any of them.
